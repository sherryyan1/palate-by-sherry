# palate by Sherry

*Good research deserves good taste.*

Create or refine an editable research presentation from existing slides, rough text or a paper. Deliver a new deck and a concise account of the changes, checks and unresolved issues when the requested work and available tools support that delivery. An uploaded deck is not a prerequisite. Use **palate** in prose and **palate by Sherry** as the full name; keep the existing `$palate` discovery and ChatGPT `@` selection behavior.

## Coordinate Sherry's Picks

Use the four capabilities as a connected design workflow. Read the relevant canonical guide before making its decisions; the [design system](references/design-system.md) is the human navigation hub, not a second set of rules.

| Capability | Responsibility and when to load it | Working outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [Read the room](references/read-the-room.md) | Establish or revisit the brief before communication decisions. Reuse supplied answers through the existing guided intake. | Audience, purpose, time, screen, depth, language and delivery decisions with their reasons. |
| [Find the thread](references/find-the-thread.md) | Diagnose the whole argument before creating or substantially refining a deck. Review the title sequence before changing order, wording or layout. | Private narrative map, question-to-evidence links, slide roles, neighbor relationships and justified retain/change decisions. |
| [Distill the message](references/distill-the-message.md) | Refine slide-level wording, titles, captions and information density within the selected argument. | Clear messages with sufficient visible method, evidence and qualifications; traceable notes/appendix decisions. |
| [Refine the visuals](references/refine-the-visuals.md) | Construct or improve every slide in scope after its explanatory job is known. Load its specialist guides for diagrams, covers, tables or figures as needed. | Source-specific visual storyboards and native compositions that make the science easier to understand. |

For a narrow correction, inspect enough context to preserve the argument and limit edits to the authorized scope. Do not turn a font repair into an unsolicited story rewrite. For broader work, explore alternatives seriously but implement only those with an identifiable benefit. An effective existing title, order or composition can remain. Never use the number of edits as a quality measure.

The capabilities interact: a new finding may change the narrative, and a visual split may require another title-sequence review. Keep the same source identities and rationale through these revisions. Primary [decision rules](references/design-decision-library.json) apply within their triggers and exceptions; contextual patterns remain choices, not compulsory templates. Scientific meaning and explicit user constraints govern every choice.

## Execute the task

1. **Inspect the material and environment.** Follow [host workflows](references/host-workflows.md) and check actual tool availability. For files, use [presentation_route.py](scripts/presentation_route.py); supply `--source-kind text` or `--source-kind paper` when that is their actual role. Read pasted text directly. A PDF extension does not determine whether it is a paper or an exported deck. Inspect source structure, scientific content and rendered pages where applicable, including notes, hidden slides, figures, equations and build states.
2. **Resolve the communication brief.** Follow [Read the room](references/read-the-room.md) and [guided intake](references/guided-intake.md): setting, external audience when applicable, then duration, venue screen and depth. Reuse answers and allow delegated design choices. Ask only consequential follow-ups. Preserve existing presentation language unless a change is requested; keep README, conversation and slide language independent. Single-page monthly updates remain outside this workflow.
3. **Establish scientific support and the story.** Read [scientific fidelity](references/scientific-fidelity.md) before content changes. Use [source-to-slides](references/source-to-slides.md) for notes and papers. Build a private claim-to-source ledger and narrative map. Perform the [Slide Title Sequence Review](references/find-the-thread.md#slide-title-sequence-review) on original or provisional titles before editing pages. Check why each slide follows its predecessor and prepares its successor, then verify the titles against their bodies and evidence. Do not manufacture novelty, conflict, causal claims or findings to create a better story. For new decks or substantial refinement, show the [task-specific execution brief](references/read-the-room.md#show-the-task-specific-execution-brief) after initial diagnosis and before changing the deck; reuse the existing plan and respect the user's review or execution instructions.
4. **Develop the explanation.** Use [Distill the message](references/distill-the-message.md) to retain the reasoning and detail this audience needs. Titles must serve the selected narrative role, not merely sound clever. Complete required scientific or translation clarifications before dependent rewriting. Resolve a material source discrepancy explicitly instead of silently selecting the more convenient claim. For prose that needs polishing, follow [language-specific companion routing](references/distill-the-message.md#language-specific-prose-polishing) when the matching skill is available, then recheck scientific meaning and layout.
5. **Storyboard and author.** Follow [Refine the visuals](references/refine-the-visuals.md) and the [outline, storyboard, construction sequence](references/visual-storytelling.md#outline-then-storyboard-then-build). Every substantive Introduction page needs a source-specific illustrated explanation; icons are compact supporting cues, not oversized substitutes for a main image. Preserve sufficient scientific detail and explanatory relationships. Use the [cover guide](references/cover-art-direction.md) for its separate composition. Resolve table colors when applicable, and follow native work-area, type, navigation, anchoring, branding and retained-text rules. For complete-deck work, [plan the final audience-facing slide](references/visual-storytelling.md#plan-the-final-audience-facing-slide), preserving an effective ending and respecting selected-slide scope or an explicit omission. Place a dedicated closing slide after the final content or conclusion slide and before appendices. A conclusion or research-summary slide must not double as the ending.
6. **Review both argument and artifact.** Re-read the final title sequence and question-to-result map after construction. Verify source coverage, numerical insights, scientific force and necessary qualifications. Separately inspect every final slide in the delivery application and verify native editability, fonts, equations, figures, notes and relevant playback. Compare changed slides with the source; for new decks, compare with the supplied research. Apply the shared [ending review](references/scientific-fidelity.md#deliver-the-result-and-explain-its-limits), including purpose, placement, duplicates, placeholders and projected readability. Verify the dedicated closing slide is not hidden/skipped and test the final native file from the summary through the closing slide; missing, misplaced or skipped required endings block final delivery. File checks and title extraction do not establish scientific, narrative or visual quality.
7. **Deliver an honest account.** Save new output files. Explain the consequential narrative and design changes, what was retained, completed checks and specific issues requiring attention. If essential authoring or native review is unavailable, identify the output as an outline or candidate and state the pending work. Briefly identify the chosen ending and its fit, or report an explicit omission or out-of-scope missing ending. Do not claim that a missing review passed.

## Use the correct native workflow

Apply the [shared native delivery contract](references/native-delivery.md) to both formats. Use [capability status](references/native-capabilities.json) to distinguish implemented operations, tested scope and pending checks. Final/candidate status follows evidence, never the format name. New Keynote creation and structural changes use [native authoring](references/keynote-authoring.md) or controlled Keynote editing; no implicit conversion is authorized.


Preserve an existing deck's application unless another delivery is requested. Text and papers have no source presentation application; reuse an explicit choice or establish PowerPoint or Keynote before authoring. Inspect the actual container because `.key` can also be a credential. Match a chosen 4:3 or 16:9 venue ratio through native reflow before defining the work area. An explicit unsure answer retains an existing canvas; without a source canvas, disclose a provisional 16:9 design and keep the venue ratio unconfirmed.

| Work | Required route and limits |
| :--- | :--- |
| Existing PowerPoint slides | Follow the [PowerPoint workflow](references/pptx-workflow.md). The conservative helper preserves existing content and supports only its bounded operations; it cannot rewrite titles, reorder the story, translate or create slides. |
| Existing Keynote slides | Follow the [native Keynote workflow](references/keynote-workflow.md) on Mac. No PowerPoint conversion is required. Bounded multi-slide fixtures passed on macOS 27.0.1 / Keynote 13.1; see [capability status](references/native-capabilities.json) for scope and remaining gaps. Live application access and deck-specific review must still succeed before claiming compatibility. |
| New slides, rewritten text, reordered or rebuilt explanations | Use an available native authoring route for the chosen application with a content ledger. For PowerPoint with the installed presentations skill, use JavaScript `@oai/artifact-tool` and its finalizer. Keynote uses available native authoring or controlled application editing. Bounded helpers do not acquire new operations because the narrative requires them. |
| Reference-based adaptation | Follow [style adaptation](references/style-adaptation.md); compare the explanation and scientific detail, not only its palette or panel arrangement. Preserve evidence and avoid copying incidental editing remnants. |
| Requested translation | Complete the [translation preflight](references/scientific-fidelity.md#scientific-translation). Resolve unclear target language or material term ambiguity before dependent rewriting. |

If the native runtime is unavailable, continue independent analysis or supported candidate work and keep the unavailable route pending. Do not silently substitute a conversion or cloud service. A requested cross-format delivery needs a separate conversion baseline and review in the delivery application.

## Runtime and safeguards

Locate bundled runtimes with `load_workspace_dependencies` when available, otherwise inspect the execution environment. Do not assume a web session provides local disk access, a presentation SDK or native applications.

| Operation | Tool and requirements |
| :--- | :--- |
| Identify source role and native route | [presentation_route.py](scripts/presentation_route.py); standard library, read-only. |
| Prepare an ordered title review | [review_title_sequence.py](scripts/review_title_sequence.py); Existing `lxml` dependency; accepts PPTX or an explicit ordered inventory for Keynote/new outlines. It does not validate or rewrite the narrative. |
| Inspect, apply, validate or finalize preserved PPTX objects | [palate_pptx.py](scripts/palate_pptx.py); `lxml`, with Pillow for raster diagnostics. Follow the [plan contract](references/transform-plan.md). |
| Extract checked raster figure regions | [extract_figure_panels.py](scripts/extract_figure_panels.py); Pillow, a source-hashed plan and fresh output directory. Follow [figure panels](references/figure-panels.md); extraction does not establish scientific completeness. |
| Export native PowerPoint previews on Windows | [render_pptx.ps1](scripts/render_pptx.ps1); installed PowerPoint, opened read-only. Other native PowerPoint workflows remain valid. |
| Inspect and adjust supported native Keynote objects | [keynote_native.py](scripts/keynote_native.py) and [keynote_native.js](scripts/keynote_native.js); macOS, Keynote and automation access. Follow the [adapter contract](references/keynote-workflow.md#native-adapter-contract) and its untested status. |

Retain original data, conclusions, units, labels, legends, uncertainty, assumptions, equations and references. Preserve complex figures when reconstruction cannot be verified. Keep interpretation-changing qualifications with the claim. Never flatten a whole slide to conceal lost editability, or bypass a rejected operation by deleting evidence or disabling guards. Check animation and media through playback where relevant. PowerPoint review does not validate Keynote, or vice versa.

Keep research inputs, outputs, narrative maps, title reviews and other task artifacts private within the authorized environment. Publishing research or transferring it to an additional service needs explicit authorization. Follow [asset sourcing](references/asset-sourcing.md) for actual access, licensing and required visible credits. Repository source, packages and installed copies synchronize explicitly; preserve local edits and follow installation requirements before replacing an existing skill.

For documentation and reports, follow [communication design](references/communication-design.md) and [writing style](references/writing-style.md). Approved README copy and navigation require explicit approval before changes; the existing links reach the design hub without adding implementation detail to the READMEs.
