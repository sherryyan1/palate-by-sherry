# Spacing and hierarchy

Spacing is a core design judgment in palate. It tells listeners which elements belong together, where an explanation begins and when attention should move to another idea. A slide can have aligned objects and ample empty area yet communicate the wrong relationships. Judge space by the scientific reading order it creates.

## External principles

Apple's identity guidelines protect a signature with clear space and explicitly allow more than the stated minimum. The applicable lesson is to give a mark room to remain distinct from competing content; Apple's numeric clear-space ratio belongs to its own identity system. ([Apple identity guidelines, page 10](https://training.apple.com/content/dam/appletraining/us/en/2022/documents/Apple_ID_Guidelines_Channel_180919.pdf#page=10))

IBM describes layout through repetition and variation: recurring dimensions, proportions and alignments create continuity, while appropriate variation gives different content a distinct role. Its guidance also connects breathing room with visual pauses and hierarchy with movement through information. These principles explain why a deck needs both local organization and rhythm across slides. ([IBM layout guidance](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/layout/tips-and-techniques/))

Carbon explains that proximity strengthens perceived association, repeated spacing patterns imply related elements of comparable importance, and surrounding space can increase prominence. It allows dense sections within a composition that still gives the eye places to rest. These relationships matter more than maximizing the amount of empty space. ([Carbon spacing guidance](https://www.carbondesignsystem.com/building-blocks/foundations/spacing/overview))

IBM's typography guidance links legibility with alignment, appropriate leading and manageable line lengths. It shows how weight, contrast and scale can distinguish titles from subtitles without accumulating redundant emphasis. Spacing therefore needs to be judged together with the typography that occupies it. ([IBM type basics](https://www.ibm.com/design/language/typography/type-basics/))

In Pentagram's redesign of *The Philadelphia Inquirer*, controlled white space and stronger organization give layouts room while allowing larger images and infographics. This is a useful editorial precedent for research: organize the page so the evidence gains clarity and presence. ([Pentagram project](https://www.pentagram.com/work/the-philadelphia-inquirer))

## Application to research slides

The following guidance adapts those principles to research presentations. Establish margins and major gaps from the content, the slide's proportions and the intended display, then express geometry in inches for the transformation plan. Brand-specific logo ratios and UI pixel or rem values belong to their original systems; they are not fixed presets for slides. When physical canvas size changes, preserve the intended proportions and type hierarchy; when aspect ratio changes, reconsider the composition. Check the result at the intended display scale.

Start with the scientific groups. Keep a figure, its relevant interpretation and essential qualifications close enough to read together, with a more distinct gap before another independent group. Leave outer breathing room so content does not compete with the slide edges. Repeat comparable gaps across comparable relationships to establish rhythm, but vary them when the content changes. A larger gap can accidentally detach a unit, label, caveat or legend from its evidence.

Use numeric alignment as the starting structure, then inspect visible content. Text boxes include internal margins and space beyond glyph bounds; letters, symbols and different type weights also carry unequal visual mass. Compare visible edges and baselines, and make modest optical adjustments where equal coordinates appear uneven. Preserve chart-axis alignment and meaningful comparison scales while doing so.

| Condition | Design response and check |
| --- | --- |
| Groups merge visually | Clarify the gap between groups; keep each group's labels and qualifications attached. |
| A heading feels disconnected | Reconsider its distance, weight and scale together; verify the intended parent-child relationship. |
| Evidence fills the slide | Preserve readable labels, units, legends and uncertainty. Reorganize or split with a content ledger when appropriate; never delete science or shrink all text to manufacture whitespace. |

Review the final render for both breathing room and association. Dense scientific evidence can justify compact spacing when each relationship remains readable and the required detail can still be inspected.
