# Closing compositions and backgrounds

Use this collection when designing the dedicated closing slide required by [visual storytelling](visual-storytelling.md#plan-the-final-audience-facing-slide). Select one or two relevant options, then inspect only the selected slide or background. The public runtime contains composition recipes and three reusable background images, not finished editable slide templates or a fixed house style. User-supplied local slide assets can extend that collection as described below. Keep the closing words, supporting line and any verified contact details native and editable in the current deck.

## Use supplied slide assets

When a user supplies a deck as reusable material, treat every authorized page as an actual slide asset, not merely a visual observation. Inspect the relevant page in the original deck and reuse its native layout, groups, illustrations and text frames on a new output copy where the intended reuse is permitted. If a private material catalog accompanies the deck, use its source identity and slide index to load only the selected page. Do not assume private assets exist in another installation or cloud workspace; report their absence rather than claiming they are bundled.

Replace sample names, email addresses, phone numbers, websites and social links with supplied or verified details, or omit those elements. Retain required attribution and check any restrictions for the intended output. A user-supplied asset's availability for local work does not establish permission to redistribute its source in a public skill package. Keep that distribution decision separate from using the supplied material; do not automatically reduce an entire supplied deck to generic recipes because only some backgrounds are publicly packaged.

For backgrounds that originated as covers, distinguish the reusable artwork from cover metadata and compose a separate closing slide. Preserve editable source objects when reusing a complete slide; do not flatten it to a screenshot. Adapt the selected asset to the deck's purpose and visual system and perform the same native review as for a newly authored ending.

## Choose a composition

The recipes are fallbacks and adaptation guidance when a supplied reusable slide is unavailable or unsuitable. They distill approved visual observations without redistributing the private reference templates, their illustrations, social icons or sample contacts. Choose the deck's typography and resolved palette; approval of a composition does not require its incidental green, red or teal colors.

| Option | Composition and hierarchy | Suitable use and limits |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Corner illustration | Large closing words at upper left; a short invitation below; one subject illustration anchored at lower right. Keep the middle open. | A friendly Q&A with a recognizable research subject. Use an original or separately licensed illustration; do not enlarge a stock icon into the main visual. |
| Geometric skyline | Compact left-aligned text balanced by a larger geometric construction at the right edge. | A graphic, architectural ending when geometry matches the deck. Construct a new native motif rather than copying a reference's distinctive artwork. |
| Illustrated scene | A short central closing message framed by a coherent scene along the sides and lower edge. | A tutorial or accessible seminar. The scene must relate to the topic and leave the closing message dominant. No scene asset is bundled. |
| Illustrated horizon | Place the invitation above a low subject-specific landscape, with substantial open space between them. | An approachable energy or environment talk. Author or license the landscape for the actual subject; avoid importing template social-media rows. |
| Organic boundary | A flowing illustration boundary on one side and a strong, short typographic block on the other. | A lively ending that fits the deck's existing visual language. The boundary is a layout choice, not a prescribed color or a copied silhouette. |
| Panoramic background | Closing words in the open upper field; a detailed landscape across the lower half. | A calm illustrated ending for a relevant regional, infrastructure or energy topic. Inspect [coastal panorama](../assets/closing-backgrounds/coastal-panorama.png) only if selected. |
| Dark infrastructure | Large light closing words on the open left field; the subject fills the right side. | A dramatic ending that can echo a related cover. Inspect [transmission tower](../assets/closing-backgrounds/transmission-tower.png) only if selected. |
| Diagonal illustration | A strong diagonal subject enters from the left; a short right-aligned closing message occupies the clear upper-right field. | A bold energy ending with an existing diagonal visual language. Inspect [solar diagonal](../assets/closing-backgrounds/solar-diagonal.png) only if selected. |
| Circular image | A left-aligned closing block, restrained separator and one large circular image at right, with a quiet inset frame if useful. | A restrained photographic ending. Supply a relevant image with verified reuse permission; no reference photograph is bundled. |

## Use the three included backgrounds

These text-free generated illustrations were approved for closing-background reuse. They originated as cover artwork; their presence does not establish that a complete closing layout has been validated. See the [asset notice](../assets/closing-backgrounds/NOTICE.md) for reuse boundaries. All three are 1672 x 941 pixels, approximately 16:9. Preserve their proportions; when changing the slide ratio, recrop deliberately and recheck the clear text field rather than stretching the picture.

| Background | Initial clear text field, as fractions of slide width and height | Treatment to evaluate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coastal panorama | x 0.20-0.80, y 0.10-0.35 | Dark deck-matched type on the light upper field; keep text above the landscape. |
| Transmission tower | x 0.07-0.44, y 0.22-0.66 | Light deck-matched type on the dark left field; keep letters away from cables and tower edges. |
| Solar diagonal | x 0.57-0.93, y 0.12-0.43 | Dark deck-matched type on the light upper-right field; keep the text block clear of the diagonal panels. |

These are starting regions, not guaranteed fit boxes. Adapt them to the actual words, fonts, projected size and deck margins. Choose a different composition when the subject is unrelated or the message cannot fit legibly. The illustrations are decorative context, not scientific evidence, verified equipment drawings or a map of actual locations. For another topic, an original illustration in a similar editorial treatment may be more suitable than reusing energy imagery.

A meaningful cover motif may return on the separate closing slide, with closing text replacing cover metadata. Do not append the cover unchanged or reuse a cover title as an ending. Omit absent contact information entirely. Keep the background as an image and all text and layout elements editable; do not flatten the finished slide. Apply the existing [native ending review](scientific-fidelity.md#deliver-the-result-and-explain-its-limits), including the transition from the conclusion and the boundary before appendices.
