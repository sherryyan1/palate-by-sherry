# Presentation contexts

These profiles provide a starting brief when the request names a setting or type of talk. They help connect the purpose of a presentation to the explanation its audience may need. The [JSON profiles](presentation-contexts.json) supply the runtime values; the design emphasis below is guidance to evaluate against the actual deck and request.

| Context | Audience | Suggested duration | Design emphasis |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| Internal research | Research group | 60 min | Make the research question, methods and evidence easy to examine and discuss. |
| Internal tutorial | Research group | 10 min | Develop the concept or mechanism in a sequence that makes the connection between steps clear. |
| Government project briefing | Project meeting participants | 30 min | Use the meeting's stated purpose to decide which results, implications and qualifications need attention. |
| Expert seminar | Expert audience | 60 min | Make the contribution clear and provide enough methods, assumptions and uncertainty for the audience to assess the evidence. |
| General-audience talk | General audience | 30 min | Define the terms and explain the mechanisms needed to understand the finding and its significance. |

Use the context named by the user rather than inferring a specific event from audience expertise alone. An external expert audience, for example, does not by itself establish that the presentation is a seminar or a government project briefing. Relevant familiarity, technical depth and purpose still come from the brief and source.

When duration is missing, offer the profile value as a suggestion and obtain the user's answer. Specific instructions always override the profile. The available time guides pacing and how fully the reasoning can be developed, but does not establish a slide count or Q&A allocation. An expert talk can still need conceptual support, and a longer presentation can still benefit from a selective account of the research.

Single-page monthly progress updates are outside this multi-slide workflow.
