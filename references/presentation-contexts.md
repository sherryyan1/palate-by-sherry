# Presentation contexts

Use these defaults when the request names a context. Specific instructions override them. The [JSON profiles](presentation-contexts.json) supply runtime values.

| Context | Audience | Default time |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| Internal research | Research group | 60 min |
| Internal tutorial | Research group | 10 min |
| Government project briefing | Project meeting participants | 30 min |
| Expert seminar | Expert audience | 60 min |
| General-audience talk | General audience | 30 min |

Obtain relevant subject familiarity, technical depth and purpose from the brief. Do not infer a slide count or Q&A allocation from duration. A long talk need not be dense, and an expert audience need not see every derivation.

Single-page monthly progress updates are outside this multi-slide workflow.
